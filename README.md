# 🚀 LaunchCraft AI

**Turn Ideas into Reality with the Power of AI.**

LaunchCraft AI is a sophisticated SaaS platform designed for founders, entrepreneurs, and visionaries. It streamlines the journey from initial concept to a validated, pitch-ready startup by automating the creation of essential business assets.

## ✨ Core Features

*   **⚡ AI Launch Wizard**: Generate a complete startup foundation in under 60 seconds.
*   **🌐 AI Landing Page Builder**: High-conversion landing pages with real-time editing and lead capture.
*   **📊 AI Pitch Deck Generator**: Professionally structured 10-slide decks designed for investors.
*   **📝 AI Business Plan Architect**: Deep strategic roadmaps, SWOT analysis, and financial outlooks.
*   **🔍 Idea Validation**: Get brutally honest, data-driven feedback on your startup concepts.
*   **📈 Lead Management**: Capture and manage potential users directly from your generated pages.

## 🛠️ Technology Stack

*   **Framework**: [Next.js 15+](https://nextjs.org/) (App Router)
*   **Language**: [TypeScript](https://www.typescriptlang.org/)
*   **Database**: [PostgreSQL](https://www.postgresql.org/) (via [Prisma ORM](https://www.prisma.io/))
*   **Styling**: [Tailwind CSS](https://tailwindcss.com/) & [Shadcn UI](https://ui.shadcn.com/)
*   **Animations**: [Framer Motion](https://www.framer.com/motion/)
*   **Authentication**: [NextAuth.js v5](https://authjs.dev/)
*   **Payments**: [Stripe](https://stripe.com/)
*   **AI Engine**: [OpenAI GPT-4 Turbo](https://openai.com/)

## 🚀 Getting Started

1.  **Clone the repository**
2.  **Install dependencies**: `npm install`
3.  **Setup environment variables**: Copy `.env.example` to `.env` and fill in your keys.
4.  **Run migrations**: `npx prisma db push`
5.  **Start development**: `npm run dev`
